# MT00_BOOTSTRAP COMMON TEMPLATE 07-13-2 LOCK

MT00_BOOTSTRAP must treat the common operation template sections 7 through 13-2 as primary bootstrap knowledge.

## Core principles

### Primary product / secondary product

Primary products are not modified.
Original information, bones, oral records, handoff origins, recovery origins, and source material are preserved.

Only the process of secondary-product creation may be edited.

### 022 / 028

022 is the fixed-bone shelf.
028 is the oral / pending / temporary shelf.
Neither should be summarized or compressed where future recovery, heat, or responsibility lines may be needed.

### Summarization / compression

What must remain must not be summarized or compressed.
Permitted actions are organization, ordering, labeling, history attachment, relocation, and消込.
Forbidden actions are summarization, compression, thinning by paraphrase, and breaking the line of source information.

### Source heat

If heat cannot survive without original wording, the original wording must remain available.
Secondary products for writing must preserve source heat and may not replace original heat with bare summaries.

### Notebook and bookshelf

The working notes may be light.
The back shelf must remain recoverable.
Derived products must trace back to source information.

### ZIP numbering

021 is the first working number.
021, 022, 024, and 028 are base lanes.
099 is for writing-pack output, not for initial project setup.

### ZIP naming

Persistent shelf ZIPs use `[number]_[short-code]_v[version]`.
50-story pack ZIPs use `099_[work-code]_[range]_v[version]` and require a work code.

## Non-goal

This lock does not authorize MT00_BOOTSTRAP to become DS90 or to design the work.
